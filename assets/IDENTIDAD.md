# Identidad FotoLista

Logo creado con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes, el 22 de octubre de 2026. PNG original con transparencia; ICO convertido desde el PNG para Windows en tamaños 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 y 256 px.

Uso: cabecera de la aplicación, portada de la vista vacía e icono de ventana. El script de empaquetado incluye los assets y asigna el ICO al ejecutable.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create a single polished app icon for FotoLista, a Windows desktop photo preparation and image compression application. Square composition, transparent canvas outside the icon. One bold rounded square tile in deep navy #111c27; within it, a distinctive mint turquoise #65dfbf stylized photograph frame with a simple mountain silhouette and small circular sun, combined elegantly with one checkmark at the lower right to mean photo ready. Flat vector-like graphic aesthetic, crisp clean edges, generous bold shapes readable at 16px, balanced optical spacing. Tile almost fills canvas with small even transparent margin. No text, no letters, no watermark, no mockup, no photographic scene, no extra alternative logos. Friendly, modern, professional desktop software identity. The same mark will be used for the header, cover area and Windows title-bar icon.
